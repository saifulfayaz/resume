Inside the project +

The design uses a full-bridge rectifier, isolated conversion through a high-frequency transformer, and an SPWM-controlled inverter.

The preliminary prototype uses an Arduino Mega 2560, IRF3205 MOSFETs, IR2110 drivers and an LC filter.

Team: Afas Yousuf, Malik Inam Ul Haq, Najmu Saqib and Saif Ul Fayaz. Supervised by Dr. Ward Ul Hijaz Paul.
